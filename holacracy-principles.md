# Holacracy® principles


## 1. Purpose structures authority, not personal aims

The organization’s Purpose is distinct from individual aims, and authority is explicitly structured and exercised in service of that Purpose.



## 2. Structure is defined by organizational functions, not people

Work, authority, and expectations are organized around explicit organizational functions that carry defined authority, not around people, positions, status, or implicit expectations.



## 3. Tensions drive evolution

Sensed gaps between current reality and potential are valid signals for adaptation, with explicit pathways for processing them into changes in work, expectations, or authority.



## 4. People have default authority within explicit boundaries

People have authority to act by default within their explicit organizational functions, with explicit limits that connect local autonomy to the whole.



## 5. Authority comes from rules, not personal power

Authority and expectations are grounded in explicit, transparent, written constitutional rules and the written records governed by those rules.
